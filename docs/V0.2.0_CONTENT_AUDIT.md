## Review procedure

For every row, the human reviewer must check all four criteria below. Mark `Checked` only when every criterion passes; otherwise leave it blank and record the required edit in review notes.

1. Editorial naturalness and suitability as a short mother-like message.
2. Safety, including the exclusions in [`CONTENT_GUIDE.md`](CONTENT_GUIDE.md).
3. Natural language, dialect, or regional English usage without exaggeration.
4. No duplicate or substantially similar wording within the bundle.

Automated validation and mechanical similarity screening help the review, but do not satisfy it. After all rows pass, rerun the content validator and record the reviewer, approval date, and final status as `approved` in this document.

Mechanical screening on 2026-07-17 compared every new candidate with all same-language messages after lower-casing and removing punctuation and whitespace. It found no pairs at or above a 0.68 character-sequence similarity threshold after candidate revisions. This threshold-based result is not a semantic review and does not clear any checkbox below.
